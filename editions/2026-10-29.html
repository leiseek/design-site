Takes - a decision map for design agents

by Ronan Flynn-Curran
AIAI

把 AI 设计协作的「过程」画成一张决策地图，用线程式画布让每次 prompt、迭代与评审都有迹可循，值得借鉴的是为 agent 工作流设计专属的可视化语言，而不只是展示最终稿。It turns the 'process' of AI design collaboration into a decision map — a threaded canvas where every prompt, iteration, and review leaves a trace. The takeaway: design a dedicated visual language for agent workflows, not just the final output.
